Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains how the apps and games published by JNK Labs (shown on Google Play as JNK Labs / JNKAPPS) handle your information. It covers Ring Unlock, Luttapi & Ghosts and SYNC: Anti-Scroll AI News. If you have any question, contact us at jnklabssupport@gmail.com.

1. Ring Unlock

What we collect

Ring Unlock has no account or login, and we (JNK Labs) do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress (level, coins, boosters, settings such as sound and vibration) is saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This may be used to show personalised ads where you have agreed to it.

  • How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Your choices and consent

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before ads use personal data. You can change your choice any time in the game's Settings → Privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Rewarded videos and purchases

Watching a rewarded video is always your choice. Ring Unlock has no in-app purchases.

2. Luttapi & Ghosts

Luttapi & Ghosts does not require an account. Game progress is stored on your device. If the game shows ads, they are provided by Google AdMob and work as described for Ring Unlock above.

3. SYNC: Anti-Scroll AI News

SYNC fetches news from the internet and uses AI services to summarise it. To do this, the app sends requests (such as the topics or categories you choose) to our servers and/or third-party AI and news providers. We do not sell your personal information. If the app shows ads, they are provided by Google AdMob as described above.

Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security and retention

Information saved on your device stays there until you uninstall the app. Information processed by advertising partners is kept according to their own policies (linked above).

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version.
